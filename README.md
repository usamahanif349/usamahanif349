### Featured projects

#### 🌫️ [Pakistan AQI Prediction](https://github.com/usamahanif349/Pakistan-AQI-Prediction): ML vs deep learning, with a research paper
Predicts air quality and health category for 10 Pakistani cities using 10 years of data. It compares classical ML, an ANN and an LSTM forecaster.
- Tuned Gradient Boosting: **R² 0.974** and **91.7% classification accuracy**, validated with 5-fold cross-validation; SHAP explains the predictions
- IEEE-format paper archived on Zenodo with a DOI and linked to my [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0001-8811-4058), plus a live Streamlit app with a city map, trends and comparisons
- `Python` `scikit-learn` `XGBoost` `TensorFlow` `SHAP` `Streamlit`

[Live app](https://pakistan-aqi-prediction-frhdsrc2ivtubpkdotcau5.streamlit.app/) · [Paper](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22859477) · [Code](https://github.com/usamahanif349/Pakistan-AQI-Prediction)

#### ⚽ [Sports Vision Tracker](https://github.com/usamahanif349/Sports-vision-tracker): player tracking and match analytics
A computer vision pipeline that tracks players and the ball in match footage and turns it into analytics.
- YOLOv8 detection with BoT-SORT tracking, and automatic team assignment by clustering jersey colours (no manual labels)
- Per-player speed and distance, positional heatmaps and a live possession bar drawn on the video
- `Python` `YOLOv8` `OpenCV` `scikit-learn`

#### 📄 [DocuQuery](https://github.com/usamahanif349/DocuQuery-RAG): multi-PDF question answering (RAG)
Upload several PDFs and ask questions; every answer cites the exact document and page it came from.
- FastEmbed embeddings with FAISS vector search, and Llama 3.3 70B through the Groq API for fast answers
- Page-level citations so answers can be checked, plus adjustable chunk size, overlap and top-k
- `Python` `LangChain` `FAISS` `Groq` `Streamlit`

[Code](https://github.com/usamahanif349/DocuQuery-RAG)

#### 📊 [Big Data Analytics projects](https://github.com/usamahanif349/big-data-analytics-course): marketing analytics for Pakistani brands
A series of course projects, each with its own repository and live dashboard:
- **[Footwear sentiment analysis](https://github.com/usamahanif349/pk-footwear-sentiment-analysis):** 881 real comments in English, Roman Urdu and Urdu, analysed with a multilingual transformer (XLM-R)
- **[Ad budget optimizer](https://github.com/usamahanif349/pk-footwear-budget-optimizer):** where to spend an ad budget, what to say and when, with Spark and Hadoop benchmarks
- `PySpark` `Hadoop` `Hugging Face` `SciPy` `Plotly` `Streamlit`
